# Order tracking and changes

## Finding your order status

Every order has a number starting with PB, shown in your confirmation email. You can see its status by signing in to your Pinebrook account and opening Orders, or by using the tracking link in your dispatch email.

## Changing an order

You can change the items or the shipping address within one hour of placing your order by emailing orders@pinebrook.example. After one hour the order is being packed and can't be changed.

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel within one hour of placing your order for a full refund. After that, the order can't be cancelled; once it arrives you can return it under our returns policy.

## Late or missing packages

If tracking shows your package as delivered but you can't find it, wait one business day, as carriers sometimes mark packages early. If it still hasn't arrived, email orders@pinebrook.example and we will open a claim with the carrier.

## Damaged on arrival

If something arrives damaged, email photos of the item and the box to orders@pinebrook.example within 7 days of delivery. We ship a replacement at no cost, and you don't need to send the damaged item back.
