# Product warranty

## What the warranty covers

Small appliances and cookware come with a 2-year limited warranty from the delivery date. It covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal home use.

## What the warranty doesn't cover

The warranty doesn't cover normal wear and tear, scratches or discoloration from use, damage from drops or misuse, commercial use, or repairs made by anyone other than Pinebrook.

## How to make a warranty claim

Email warranty@pinebrook.example with your order number, a short description of the problem, and a photo or video that shows it. We reply within two business days.

## Repair, replacement or refund

If the claim is approved, we repair or replace the item, and ship the replacement free of charge. If the item is no longer sold, we refund what you paid for it.

## Warranty or return

In the first 30 days after delivery, use the returns process instead of a warranty claim. After 30 days, faulty items are handled under the warranty.
