# Returns and refunds

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. The 30 days start on the delivery date shown in tracking.

## Used and opened items

Small appliances, such as kettles, toasters and blenders, can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Used items are accepted if undamaged, minus a 10% restocking fee. Unopened items in their original packaging have no restocking fee.

## Items we can't take back

We can't accept returns of gift cards, pantry and food items, knives that have been sharpened, or anything marked final sale.

## How to start a return

Email returns@pinebrook.example with your order number, which starts with PB. We reply within one business day with return instructions. Customers in the United States get a prepaid return label. Customers in Canada and the United Kingdom pay for return shipping.

## Refund timing

We issue refunds within 5 business days of receiving your return. Your bank may take a further 3 to 10 business days to show the refund. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

## Exchanges

We don't do direct exchanges. To swap an item, return it for a refund and place a new order.
