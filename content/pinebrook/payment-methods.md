# Payment methods

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Pinebrook gift cards.

## When you're charged

Your card is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order is dispatched. If part of an order ships later, you're only charged for what has shipped.

## Currency

All prices are in US dollars. Customers in Canada and the United Kingdom are charged in US dollars, and their bank converts the amount at its own rate.

## Gift cards

Pinebrook gift cards never expire and can be used on any order. They can't be exchanged for cash, and a lost gift card can only be replaced if you have the purchase receipt.

## Payment security

Payments are handled by our payment processor. Pinebrook never sees or stores your full card number.
