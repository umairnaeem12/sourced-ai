# Shipping rates and delivery times

## Where we ship

We ship to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom. We can't ship to any other country yet, and we can't deliver to freight forwarders.

## Processing time

We dispatch every order within two business days of payment. Orders placed on a weekend or a US public holiday are dispatched within two business days of the next working day.

## Delivery times

After dispatch, standard shipping within the United States takes 3 to 5 business days and express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. International orders to Canada arrive in 5 to 8 business days. Orders to the United Kingdom arrive in 7 to 10 business days.

## Shipping rates

Standard shipping within the United States costs $6.95 and is free on orders over $75. Express shipping within the United States costs $18. Shipping to Canada is a flat $14.95 and is free on orders over $150. Shipping to the United Kingdom is a flat $24.

## Tracking your shipment

When your order is dispatched we email you a tracking link. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Customs and import duties

Orders to Canada and the United Kingdom may be charged import duties or taxes by the carrier on delivery. These charges are set by the destination country and are paid by the customer. We can't refund duties on returned items.
